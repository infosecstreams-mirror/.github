# Welcome to the InfoSec Streams Mirror (UK) 🇬🇧

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-infosecstreams.wupinyin.co.uk-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://infosecstreams.wupinyin.co.uk)

We are a UK-based, community-maintained mirror of the popular InfoSec Streams project. Our goal is to provide a frictionless, accessible, and fast-updating directory of the best cybersecurity, hacking, and OSINT content creators across Twitch and YouTube.

---

## 🚀 Why we exist
This mirror was created and is actively maintained by **[Wupinyin](https://github.com/DiscoMouse)** to offer exclusive, custom features for the InfoSec streaming community:
- 🏆 **The Top 10 Leaderboard**: A dynamic Chart.js visualization of the most active streamers.
- ⚡ **Frictionless Onboarding**: We ditched the tedious Pull Request requirements. Streamers can get added to the list simply by letting us know via Issues!
- 🔒 **Self-Hosted Privacy**: We removed third-party analytics and external CDNs. All assets are self-hosted to respect the privacy of the privacy-conscious InfoSec community.

## 🤝 Getting Involved

We welcome contributions! Whether you want to add yourself to the directory or submit a patch to our custom features:
1. Check out our **[Issues](https://github.com/infosecstreams-mirror/infosecstreams-mirror.github.io/issues)** tab to request addition to the streamers list.
2. If you find a bug in our custom leaderboard or tracking, feel free to open a ticket!

*Note: For upstream Golang scrapers (`secinfo`, `streamstatus`), please refer to the original community repositories. We maintain our own frontend and static lists.*
